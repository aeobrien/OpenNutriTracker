# Build 48 — the packet you just typed in can go straight on your day

**22 August 2026**

## What changed

When you save a packet — photographed or typed in by hand — the message that
says it is in the household food list now comes with a button: **Put it on
today**.

Press it and you land on the app's normal portion screen for that food, on the
meal the clock suggests. You still say how much; the app does not guess, because
a packet tells you what is in a hundred grams and nothing at all about how much
of it you ate.

Don't press it and nothing happens. The food is in the list either way. Entering
a packet and eating a packet are two different things — typing in a jar of coffee
as you put it in the cupboard doesn't mean you drank it — so your day is never
touched unless you ask.

## What it does if the Mac Mini is asleep

It tells you, in those words, and it tells you the food is safe in the list. That
second half is deliberate: at that exact moment the thing you actually want to
know is whether the packet you just finished typing in has gone anywhere.

## Where the offer appears

After photographing a packet, and after typing one in by hand. Not when you find
a food through the search box — there, the next thing you do anyway is tap the
food you just confirmed, so an offer would only duplicate the step in front of
you.

## Also worth knowing

Nothing else changed. Everything from build 47 is as it was.

Behind this build I checked the whole plan against the code rather than building
from the top of it. Thirty of its thirty-three pieces were already built and
tested; this was one of the two that genuinely weren't. The third — being able to
say you ate tonight's planned dinner from your phone — is waiting on you, because
it builds onto the row you asked me to take off the Home screen on 20 August.
