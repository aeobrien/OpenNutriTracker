# Build 57 — a way into the planning screens, and four of your answers

**Date:** 2026-08-23 (times below are read from the clock in the turn they are
written)

Build 56 is replaced. Everything in 56 is in 57. What follows is what is new,
and it is almost entirely your seven answers from this morning being built.

## A Plan tab, at the bottom of the screen

> "Yes, give them their own menu item on the bottom of the screen."

There is a fifth item on the bar now, between Diary and Recipes, called Plan.
It opens the week-ahead panel — the one that was taken off Home on 20 August —
and through it the four release-6 pieces that had no door: a meal's own screen,
building a meal from its parts, the calorie figure worked out from those parts,
and your own share of a meal.

This does not put the week back on Home. Home is unchanged.

## A meal you plan starts as the whole of it, and says so

> "100% of the meal, but prompts you to update it."

Put a meal on a day and the whole of it goes down as yours. Under the day it
says **"Put down as all yours. Open it to change whose share it is."**

Nobody else is given a share. Writing a nought against Emily would be this
phone claiming she is not eating it, which nobody has said; leaving it unset
says nobody has said, which is true. A meal's stored figure *is* one standard
portion, so the whole of it is one portion — there is no separate idea of a
meal's size to take a percentage of.

## Nothing in the meal builder arrives ticked

> "No default, I do it manually - the learning system is unproven."

This was already true and nothing held it to it, so it is held now. The Mac
Mini still puts what you have cooked before at the front of each list — that is
a suggestion, and it is what makes four short lists faster than typing a name.
It does not tick anything.

## Half a shared meal will not move onto whoever has the other half

Log something "for both of us" and there are two rows: yours and Emily's. Open
yours, switch it to Emily's day, and it now says:

> The other half of this meal is already on Emily's day. Moving this half there
> would count one dinner twice against them and leave nobody with the other
> half.

with two ways out: **Delete it instead** — in case the meal was never really
shared and this half should not be on anybody's day — or **Leave it as it is**.

Two machines refuse it. The Mac Mini's refusal is the authoritative one,
because it is the only machine that can see both halves; but its answer comes
back through the queue, some time after the screen has gone and after the row
has already left your day. So the phone asks first, and you hear it while you
are still looking at the row.

If the Mac Mini cannot be reached, the move is refused rather than allowed. The
mistake this prevents cannot be seen once it has been made, so an unanswered
question is not treated as "nobody has it".

## On the Mac Mini, not on the phone

**The shopping list reaches fourteen days**, which is now your decision rather
than the kitchen panel's habit. It is not a ceiling — a longer stretch asked
for out loud is still obeyed.

**The batch screen's change is live on the panel**, and there is a way to see it
in the walkthrough. It is not on your phone; it is the panel's own screen.

## What is not here

Nothing about release 8's code has been re-opened. Your rule — *"If it's not
tested and approved by me, it's not done"* — is written into the project's own
record, and what it says about release 8 is that the gap is your approval, not
the build.

1008 tests on the phone, 714 on the Mac Mini, 21 on the panel. All passing.
That is evidence, not a verdict; the verdict is yours.
